Meet Juliette

Juliette is the heart of Yoga High. With training in yoga teaching, holistic massage and restorative movement, she brings a calm, attentive approach to every session.

She works with people of all levels, from beginners to experienced practitioners, helping you move with ease, release tension and feel more connected to your body.

Training & Experience

  • 200-hour Yoga Teacher Training (RYT)
  • Holistic Massage Diploma
  • Restorative yoga and nervous-system-informed care
  • Experience supporting clients with stress, burnout, and recovery

Juliette believes that movement, breath and touch can be gentle and powerful at the same time. Her sessions are designed to meet you where you are and support your wellbeing with care.
