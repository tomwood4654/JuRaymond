FAQ

What should I bring to a class?

Bring a yoga mat, water bottle, and layers you can move comfortably in. If you don’t have a mat, please ask and we can help arrange one.

Do I need experience?

No experience required. Yoga High classes are welcoming to beginners and those returning to practice, with options to adapt every movement.

How do I book?

Use the contact page to send a booking request or ask any questions. You can also message us via Instagram for quick updates.

Where is The Treat Seat?

The Treat Seat is available as an extra service alongside classes. Details on location and sessions are available through the contact page.
