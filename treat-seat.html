The Treat Seat

The Treat Seat is Yoga High’s dedicated space for restorative sessions, gentle bodywork and deep relaxation. It’s designed for time out, healing and mindful support.

Whether you want to ease tension after a long week, explore gentle movement, or experience a nourishing massage, The Treat Seat is a calm, welcoming environment that honors your pace.

What to expect

  • Restorative yoga and breath-led movement
  • Holistic massage to release tightness and invite ease
  • A tranquil setting focused on comfort and recovery
  • Personalised sessions shaped around your needs

Book a Treat Seat session today and discover a slower, more restorative way to care for your body and mind.
