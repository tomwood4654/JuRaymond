About Ju

Hi, I’m Ju. I offer yoga and holistic massage to support your whole self—body, breath, and mind. My approach is gentle, grounded, and responsive to what you need on the day.

I combine elements of Hatha, restorative and mindful movement with simple breathwork and relaxation practices. In massage, I blend Swedish, deep tissue and aromatherapy techniques to relieve tension and encourage rest.

Training & Approach

  • 200hr Yoga Teacher Training (RYT)
  • Holistic Massage Diploma
  • Further study in restorative practices and nervous-system-informed care

Whether you’re brand new or returning to practice, sessions are adapted to you—your energy, your goals, your life. You don’t have to be flexible to begin.
